Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, nightlife, seafood, and Portuguese culture.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - Famous beach in Goa
  2. Fort Aguada - Historic Portuguese fort
  3. Dudhsagar Falls - Beautiful waterfall
  4. Anjuna Beach - Popular for parties
  5. Basilica of Bom Jesus - Famous church

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry
  • Prawn Balchao
  • Vindaloo
  • Bebinca
  • Feni
