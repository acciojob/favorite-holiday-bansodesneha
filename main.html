Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India, known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious seafood. It is a perfect place for relaxation and adventure.

  1. Baga Beach – Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Fort Aguada – A historic Portuguese fort with scenic views.
  3. Dudhsagar Waterfalls – One of the tallest waterfalls in India.
  4. Basilica of Bom Jesus – A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  5. Anjuna Flea Market – Popular for shopping and local culture.
  • Goan Fish Curry – Traditional spicy seafood dish.
  • Prawn Balchão – A flavorful prawn pickle curry.
  • Bebinca – Famous Goan layered dessert.
  • Chicken Cafreal – A spicy Portuguese-inspired chicken dish.
  • Feni – Traditional Goan cashew drink.
